📄 EcoClean

EcoClean

Frontend · Backend · Deployed

Pakistan has trash pickers in almost every city, yet litter and overflowing bins are still everywhere — the collection system exists but doesn't actually work. EcoClean is a full-stack platform designed to fix that by directly connecting the three sides of the problem: trash pickers, homeowners who need the service, and business/government-level stakeholders who could scale it.

How it works:

  • Trash pickers are assigned duties with fixed time ranges/routes to pick up from
  • Homeowners can rate and review pickup service
  • A global complaint system flags failures in the process
  • Collected trash routes onward to recycling companies or other downstream partners
  • Gives trash pickers a defined role, job security, and a better, trackable workflow
  • Designed as a generalized (gen-1) system usable by everyone in the chain

Originally hosted on Vercel (frontend) and Railway (backend); currently being redeployed via GitHub after reliability issues with that hosting setup.
